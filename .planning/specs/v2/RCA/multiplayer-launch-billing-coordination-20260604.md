### 4. V2-alignment delta
Under V2 doctrine there should be ONE launch mechanism with confirm-before-bill: dispatch → closed-loop verify (`GameStateUpdate(Running)`) → green-light → bill. SP already has this (`heart_v2.rs launch_real` → `dispatch_launch_to_agent` + `abandon_launch`). MP has a parallel, older mechanism (`start_ac_server` direct `agent_senders.send`) with: bill-on-Live-poll (not confirm-before-bill at dispatch), no per-pod dispatch ACK, no abandon-refund on launch failure, debit-at-accept (before any pod confirmed). The delta: **MP coordinated-billing must reuse the heart_v2 dispatch-verify + abandon primitives** so a pod that fails to launch frees the pod AND refunds, and billing for the group starts only after the all-pods-verified gate — converging the two mechanisms. `PodSession.lobby_id` is the existing-but-unused hook to carry the group through heart_v2.

### 5. Proposed change (smallest-reversible-first, V2-framed)
Build the loop incrementally, each slice independently testable and reversible (see `slices`). Foundational slices (anything touching `waiting_for_game`/`multiplayer_waiting`/wallet/`group_session` status/dispatch) are authored + staged as a PR for Captain merge — never self-merged. The single shippable-now non-foundational slice is the booking-time acServer.exe pre-flight (read-only validation, no money mutation), extracted from the existing `ac_server.rs:144-155` check into a pure function called at booking confirmation so the failure surfaces at booking, not race-start. Temporary V1-retention justified: the direct-dispatch MP mechanism stays until the heart_v2-convergence slice lands (retire-trigger: convergence slice merged + MP e2e green on a real pod).
