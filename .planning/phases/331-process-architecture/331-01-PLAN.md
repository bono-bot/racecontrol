<objective>
Create a single `spawn_safe()` helper in rc-common that replaces all 4 duplicate `hidden_cmd()` functions and ensures every `Command::new()` call in rc-agent that spawns a background utility (taskkill, powershell, reg, netstat, tasklist, etc.) uses Stdio::null + CREATE_NO_WINDOW consistently.

Purpose: After `FreeConsole()` at startup (main.rs), inherited stdio handles are INVALID. Any `Command::new()` without `Stdio::null()` risks `ERROR_INVALID_HANDLE (os error 6)`. Currently only 15 of 76 call sites have Stdio::null -- this has caused repeated bugs, each fixed ad-hoc. This plan fixes it at the root.

Output: `spawn_safe.rs` in rc-common, all 76 Command::new sites migrated or explicitly marked as exceptions, 4 hidden_cmd() copies deleted.
</objective>
